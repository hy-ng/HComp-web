## Workshops (November 6th)

For a workshop description, please take a look at the complete information [here](https://www.humancomputation.com/attend.html#workshops).

HCOMP 2023 workshops will be held November 6, just prior to the technical conference. The HCOMP 2023 workshop fee includes attendance at any workshop, as well as breaks and lunch on the workshop day. Please register for your primary workshop only, but you may move between workshops, if desired. Workshops are individually priced. A discount is applied for main conference registrants. Individuals wishing to attend the workshops only should select the Workshop Only option for their registration type. The workshop only fees do NOT include admittance to any HCOMP 2023 technical sessions, the opening dinner, or the poster session.

## Refund Requests
The deadline for refund requests is October 6, 2023. All refund requests must be made in writing to AAAI at hcompreg@aaai.org. A $50.00 processing fee will be assessed for all refunds.

## ONSITE REGISTRATION

Preregistration is required prior to arrival onsite by registering online. Onsite registration will be held in the hall of the conference event, November 6-10. All attendees must pick up their badges for admittance to programs.

Registration hours are scheduled for Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday from 8:00am – 5:00pm; and on Thursday, November 10 from 8:00am – 12:00pm. Registration hours are subject to change depending on the final program.
