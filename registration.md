# HCOMP-21 Registration
{:.sub-page-header}
&nbsp;
{:.sub-page-border}

The Ninth AAAI Conference on Human Computation and Crowdsourcing<br>
November 14–18, 2021<br>
Virtual

**Online registration is available at [https://aaaiconf.cventevents.com/hcomp21](https://aaaiconf.cventevents.com/hcomp21)**

The HCOMP-21 technical conference registration fee includes admission to all prerecorded and interactive technical sessions, CrowdCamp, and access to the electronic version of the HCOMP-21 Conference Proceedings. While registration will remain open after the early registration deadline, we encourage all to register by October 15 to facilitate organizational planning.

## HCOMP-21 Registration Fees

Early Registration Deadline: October 15

- $150  AAAI Member
- $100  AAAI Student Member
- $175  Nonmember
- $125  Nonmember Student

### Silver
Includes discounted conference registration, plus a one-year online new or renewed membership in AAAI.
- $249  Silver Regular
- $149  Silver Student

## Registration / Proof of Student Status
To register online, please complete the form at [https://aaaiconf.cventevents.com/hcomp21](https://aaaiconf.cventevents.com/hcomp21). Students will be required to submit proof of student status during the registration process.

## Refund Requests
The deadline for refund requests is October 22, 2021. All refund requests must be made in writing to AAAI at **hcomp21@aaai.org**. A $50.00 processing fee will be assessed for all technical program refunds.
