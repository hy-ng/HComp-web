# HCOMP-20 Registration
{:.sub-page-header}
&nbsp;
{:.sub-page-border}

Online registration is available [here](https://aaaiconf.cventevents.com/event/c73c35d8-9a40-442c-93cb-3844a26404e7).

The HCOMP-20 technical conference registration fee includes admission to all prerecorded and interactive technical sessions, the workshop program, and access to the electronic version of the HCOMP-20 Conference Proceedings. While registration will remain open after the early registration deadline, we encourage all to register by September 25 to facilitate organizational planning.

## HCOMP-20 Registration Fees

Early Registration Deadline: September 25

- $150  AAAI Member
- $100  AAAI Student Member
- $175  Nonmember
- $125  Nonmember Student

### Silver
Includes discounted conference registration, plus a one-year online new or renewed membership in AAAI.
- $249  Silver Regular
- $149  Silver Student

## Workshop Program

Workshop Information can be found [here](https://www.humancomputation.com/workshops.html).

HCOMP-20 workshops will be held Monday, October 26, just prior to the technical conference. Workshops will run concurrently at 3:00 - 6:30 PM (CET - UTC+1). Technical registrants may sign up for workshops as part of their technical registration. For those wishing to attend only the Workshop program, a Workshop Only registration is offered.

Workshop Only Fee:
- $50  Regular
- $35  Student

## Registration / Proof of Student Status
To register online, please complete the form at [found here](https://aaaiconf.cventevents.com/event/c73c35d8-9a40-442c-93cb-3844a26404e7).
Students will be required to submit proof of student status during the registration process.

## Refund Requests
The deadline for refund requests is October 2, 2020. All refund requests must be made in writing to AAAI at hcomp20@aaai.org. A $50.00 processing fee will be assessed for all technical program refunds.
