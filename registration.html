HCOMP 2019 workshops will be held October 28, just prior to the technical conference. The HCOMP 2019 workshop fee includes attendance at any workshop, as well as breakfast and lunch on the workshop day. Please register for your primary workshop only, but you may move between workshops, if desired. Workshops are individually priced. A discount is applied for main conference registrants. Individuals wishing to attend the workshops only should select the Workshop Only option for their registration type. The workshop only fees do NOT include admittance to any HCOMP 2019 technical sessions, the opening dinner, or the poster session.

  • Registration / Proof of Student Status

To register online, please complete the form at https://aaaiconf.cvent.com/hcomp19. If you qualify for student rates, you will be required to submit proof of student status (PDF) through the online system before you can complete your registration.

  • Refund Requests

The deadline for refund requests is October 4, 2019. All refund requests must be made in writing to AAAI at hcomp19@aaai.org. A $100.00 processing fee will be assessed for all refunds.

  • Onsite Registration

Preregistration is required prior to arrival onsite by registering online. Onsite registration will be held in the foyer of the Skamania Lodge meeting room, October 28-30. All attendees must pick up their badges for admittance to programs.

Registration hours are scheduled for Monday, October 28 from 8:00am – 5:00pm; and Tuesday and Wednesday, October 29-30, 8:00am – 5:00pm. Registration hours are subject to change depending on the final program.
