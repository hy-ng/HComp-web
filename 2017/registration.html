  • Opening Reception: Additional Guests

Admittance to the HCOMP-17 Opening Reception (October 24) is included in the HCOMP-17 technical conference registration. Guests are welcome to attend for an additional $40.00 fee.

  • HCOMP-17 Banquet

Admittance to the HCOMP-17 Banquet at The Garrison Club (October 25) is included in the HCOMP-17 technical conference registration. Guests are welcome to attend for an additional $65.00 fee.

  • 2017 HCOMP Proceedings

Pre-registrants may order a hard copy of the HCOMP-17 proceedings, which will be mailed after the conference. The preconference discounted price will be determined in fall 2017. Shipping and handling will be added. Individuals who select this option will be contacted regarding the final cost after the conference.

  • Registration / Proof of Student Status

To register online, please complete the form at http://www.regonline.com/hcomp17. If you qualify for student rates, you will be required to submit proof of student status (PDF) through the online system before you can complete your registration.

  • Refund Requests

The deadline for refund requests is September 29, 2017. All refund requests must be made in writing to AAAI at hcomp17@aaai.org. A $100.00 processing fee will be assessed for all refunds.
