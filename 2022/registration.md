# HCOMP-22 Registration
{:.sub-page-header}
&nbsp;
{:.sub-page-border}

The Tenth AAAI Conference on Human Computation and Crowdsourcing<br>
November 6–10, 2022<br>
Virtual

**Online registration is available at: [https://aaaiconf.cventevents.com/HCOMP-22](https://aaaiconf.cventevents.com/HCOMP-22)**

The HCOMP-22 technical conference registration fee includes admission to all prerecorded and interactive technical sessions, CrowdCamp, and access to the electronic version of the HCOMP-22 Conference Proceedings. While registration will remain open after the early registration deadline, we encourage all to register by October 7 to facilitate organizational planning.

## REGISTRATION / PROOF OF STUDENT STATUS
To register online, please complete the form at [https://aaaiconf.cventevents.com/HCOMP-22](https://aaaiconf.cventevents.com/HCOMP-22). Students will be required to submit proof of student status during the registration process.

## Refund Requests
The deadline for refund requests is October 7, 2022. All refund requests must be made in writing to AAAI at hcomp22@aaai.org. A $50.00 processing fee will be assessed for all technical program refunds.
