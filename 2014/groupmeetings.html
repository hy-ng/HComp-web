Group Meetings at HCOMP:
We encourage attendees to organize informal group meetings around particular topics of interest outside of the official HCOMP program at area restaurants or other locations. The following meetings are open to interested conference attendees (but not organized by AAAI):

Citizen Science Gathering
We are organizing an informal gathering of HCOMP attendees who are interested in citizen science. The goal of the gathering is sharing ideas and progress around citizen science projects and creating a community of citizen science enthusiasts. All HCOMP attendees are welcome.
Organizers: Stuart Lynn, Edith Law, Ece Kamar
Meeting time: Friday 11/8, 6:45pm
Location: meet in the plenary room; then proceed to Matchbox, 155 S Palm Canyon Dr
More information: Please RSVP by November 6th Wednesday if you are attending by emailing to eckamar@microsoft.com

Human Computation Journal Meeting
The editorial board of the forthcoming journal Human Computation seeks an open meeting with interested colleagues at HCOMP to convey the purpose and scope of the journal and collect feedback to ensure that the journal best serves the community's goals.
